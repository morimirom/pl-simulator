    How it works

    Ratings from history

    Each club gets an attack and a defence rating from goals scored and conceded per game, relative to the league average. 2025/26 counts fully, 2024/25 at 40%, and every club is pulled slightly toward average. Hull and Coventry start from a typical promoted-club baseline.

    Form at each matchweek

    When you pick a past matchweek, the model only knows results up to that week. Those results are blended in at the weight you set, then the rest of the season is played out. That is the projection it would have made at the time.

    Future matchweeks

    From today, every simulated season follows the real fixture list. Pick a future week to see where clubs are expected to stand after it, with ranges, not just the final table.

    Playing the matches

    Expected goals = league home or away scoring rate × attacker's attack × defender's defence. Goals are drawn from a Poisson distribution. Ties in the table split on goal difference, then goals scored.
